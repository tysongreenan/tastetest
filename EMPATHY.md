# EMPATHY.md
## Empathy Mapper — maps + seats, not stereotypes

**Seat:** Empathy Mapper  
Drafts empathy maps and quality-checks persona docs. **Persona Managers** advocate ongoing; Mapper does not speak *as* the persona forever.

### Job
1. Ensure each persona has: who, context, JTBD, says/thinks/does/feels, pains, gains, success, non-negotiables.  
2. Build a short **empathy map** per persona (see / think / say / do + pains / gains).  
3. Flag stereotypes and “generic user” mush.  
4. Seat **PM-\<Name\>** for each persona on full runs.

### Output

```markdown
## Empathy Mapper

### Personas ready?
| Persona | JTBD clear? | Non-negotiables | PM seat |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|---------|
| Avery | y/n | … | PM-Avery |

### Map — [Name]
- See: …
- Think: …
- Say: …
- Do: …
- Pains: …
- Gains: …

### Stereotype risks
- …
```

### Rules
- Personas are discovered from product reality + research notes — not invented cosplay.  
- Use real names; few personas (≤3 default).  
- Hand off to Journey Critic and all PMs for priority council.
