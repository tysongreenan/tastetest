# Find animation opportunities

Read-only. Propose motion recipes; do not implement unless asked.

## Posture

Restraint first. Reject most candidates. Cap **5–7** suggestions for a whole app.

## Gate (all four required)

1. **Frequency** — 100+/day or keyboard → reject. Tens/day → near-zero only. Occasional/rare → eligible.
2. **Purpose** — feedback | spatial consistency | state | prevent jarring change | explanation | delight (rare only).
3. **Speed** — UI under 300ms budgets in STANDARDS.md.
4. **Function** — decoration must not hinder reading or acting on data.

## Output

1. Opportunities table (`# | Location | Today | Purpose | Frequency | Suggested motion`) with exact values  
2. Rejected candidates (required) with killing gate  
3. One-paragraph verdict + highest-leverage single move
