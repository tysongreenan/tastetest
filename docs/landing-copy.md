## Problem

**Title**  
Your code works. Your product still feels off.

AI coding agents are great at writing UI.  
They’re much worse at making it feel obvious, trustworthy, and effortless.

Most products end up with:

- Weak visual hierarchy
- Inconsistent components
- Confusing empty and error states
- Microcopy that makes people think
- Flows that look fine in isolation but feel broken as a whole

TasteTest exists to catch exactly these problems.

---

## What It Checks

### Don’t Make Me Think
Design should be self-evident. Clarity beats cleverness. Conventions are your friends. Omit needless words.

### Fluidity & Professionalism
Clear visual hierarchy. Consistent spacing. Strong primary actions. High-quality empty/loading/error states. Human microcopy.

### Consistency & Standards
Design system adherence. Familiar patterns.

### Cognitive Load & Trust
Recognition over recall. Progressive disclosure. Clear system status.

### Accessibility Baseline
Focus states, labels, contrast, keyboard support.
